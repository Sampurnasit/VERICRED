## 1. Zero-Knowledge Subsystem Vision

The signature capability of VERICRED is enabling a credential holder to mathematically prove satisfaction of an academic requirement (e.g., "my CGPA is at least 8.0") without disclosing their exact grade, transcript, or personal identifying attributes.

Crucially, VERICRED rejects naive circuits that merely prove knowledge of "some number greater than 8." The VERICRED ZK architecture binds the private academic claim directly to an **authentic digital signature generated by an authorized institution**.

## 2. Comprehensive Workflow Pipeline

1. **Policy Specification**: A corporate recruiter or university specifies an eligibility condition:
   $$\text{CGPA} \ge 8.0 \implies T = 800$$
2. **Witness Preparation**: The student's browser retrieves the private credential subject attributes:
   - Scaled grade: $x = 872$
   - Blinding salt: $s$
   - Institutional signature: $(R_{8x}, R_{8y}, S)$
   - Commitment: $C = \text{Poseidon}(872, s)$
   - Issuer public key: $(A_x, A_y)$
3. **In-Browser Witness Calculation**: The student client loads `CgpaThresholdValidator.wasm` in a dedicated Web Worker and calculates the circuit wire assignments.
4. **Groth16 Proof Synthesis**: `snarkjs.groth16.fullProve` processes the witness against `CgpaThresholdValidator.zkey` to produce Groth16 proof vectors $(\pi_a, \pi_b, \pi_c)$.
5. **Presentation Construction**: The student bundles the Groth16 proof with public signals $[C, T, A_x, A_y]$.
6. **Verifier Inspection**:
   - The verifier executes `snarkjs.groth16.verify(verification_key, publicSignals, proof)` in $< 10\,\text{ms}$.
   - The verifier queries `InstitutionRegistry.sol` to confirm $(A_x, A_y)$ belongs to an approved, active institution.
   - The verifier queries `RevocationRegistry.sol` to confirm commitment $C$ has not been revoked.
7. **Decision**: If all checks succeed, the portal renders a green `ELIGIBILITY PROVEN` certificate.

## 3. Core Architectural Subsystems

| Component | Technology | Location | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Circuit Compiler** | Circom 2.1.8+ | `zk/circuits/` | Compiles arithmetic constraints into R1CS |
| **Ceremony Setup** | snarkjs / Powers of Tau | `zk/scripts/` | Generates proving key (`.zkey`) and verification key (`.vkey.json`) |
| **Client Prover** | snarkjs / WebAssembly | `apps/web/` | Computes witness and Groth16 proof in student browser |
| **Verification Portal**| snarkjs / Ethers.js | `apps/web/`, `apps/api/` | Verifies pairing check and binds public signals to EVM state |
| **Cryptographic Hash** | Poseidon (2-input & 5-input) | `@vericred/crypto` | Zero-knowledge friendly algebraic hashing |
| **Elliptic Curve** | BabyJubjub over BN254 | `@vericred/crypto` | Native twisted Edwards curve for in-circuit EdDSA |
